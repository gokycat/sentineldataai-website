Frequently Asked Questions

What is enterprise AI governance?
Evaluating every AI request against organizational policy before it reaches users or systems of record. DataSentinel returns fail-closed allow or deny decisions with full audit trails.
How does AI work safely with IBM Db2?
AI interprets intent and orchestrates workflows; deterministic rules enforce policy on Db2. No SQL reaches your database without passing the governance gateway.
Who built DataSentinel?
Sentinel Data and AI Consulting, led by Robert D. Proffitt — IBM Gold Consultant for Data & AI, IBM Champion, and IBM Certified Db2 v12.1 DBA Professional.
What makes Sentinel different from prompt guardrails?
Sentinel uses a deterministic policy engine with explainable decisions and complete audit trails — not probabilistic prompt filtering that is difficult to audit under enterprise compliance requirements.
